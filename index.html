Homework

Build, record, submit.

Fourteen sheets are linked here. Session 08 is midterm week, so it intentionally has no graded homework.

Your submission in three steps

  1. Read & build. The sheet's requirements and rubric are the contract. Weekly course milestones are separate practice checkpoints.
  2. Test & explain. Check every requirement, then follow the sheet's Video Checklist in a 1–2 minute screen-and-voice demo.
  3. Submit both links. Push the source files to the correctly named GitHub repository, put the viewable video URL in its README.md, and enter the repository and video links in the class Google Sheet. The exact naming, Sheet URL, deadline, and access settings come from Telegram.
