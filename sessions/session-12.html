  4. 04Homework · The assignment

    Web Application Development

    Product management system — categories, products, uploads, pagination.

    Build a complete Product Management System — a full-featured mini web application with categories and products. This assignment brings together everything learned so far: database design with foreign keys, full CRUD operations, file uploads, pagination, search/filter, form validation, and responsive styling. You will build a real application from scratch (or on top of your Homework 11 MVC structure).

    Due: Sunday 23:59 via LMS | File: homework-12.zip containing product_app/

    Open homework sheet
  5. 05Homework · How to do it

    Work the task

    1. Read the sheet first — Overview, Requirements, and the grading rubric are the contract you are marked against.
    2. Build the task in your XAMPP htdocs folder until it runs at http://localhost/… without errors.
    3. Test against every functional and technical requirement, then use the sheet's Tips & Resources when stuck.
    4. Rehearse the Video Checklist — What to Show at the bottom of the sheet; it is exactly what you will record.
    Open requirements & tips
  6. 06Homework · How to submit

    Submit on LMS

    1. Part 1 — Code ZIP. Zip the finished file/folder using the filename specified in the homework sheet and upload it to the LMS assignment.
    2. Part 2 — Video link. Record 1–2 minutes with OBS (screen + voice), upload to YouTube as Unlisted, add it to your playlist «INS3064 — Homework — Your Name», and paste the URL into the LMS "Video Link" field — never the video file.
    3. Deadline: Sunday 23:59. Late work is marked until Monday 23:59 at −20%; after that it scores 0. Test your link in an incognito window first.
